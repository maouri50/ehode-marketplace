# Newsletter signup verification

The voluntary newsletter form was checked on desktop and at a real 390 × 844 phone viewport. The phone layout uses a single full-width input and subscription button stack.

The public form accepted a valid disposable address and displayed the generic success message. A second submission of the same address returned the identical success message and created no duplicate record. A malformed address was rejected by the email field. The two disposable QA records were deleted after verification; no campaign email was sent during the checks.
