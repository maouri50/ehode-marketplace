# Product research sources

## Etsy demand signals

Etsy’s 2025 seller trend report uses Etsy search and sales data and highlights shopper interest in personalized, sentimental gifting; entertaining and home-hosting; playful self-expression; and cozy home updates. It recommends developing inventory from trend-relevant aesthetics, keywords, and shopper interests while staying authentic to the shop’s story. The report gives examples of rising searches around table settings, glassware, cocktail napkins, colorful accessories, maximalist prints, and nostalgic home aesthetics.

Source: [Etsy Seller Trend Report: Fall and Winter 2025](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1417223353768)

Etsy’s best-selling digital-products market page frames planners and business guides as prominent digital-download categories, although the marketplace page is not a sales-rank dataset.

Source: [Etsy: Best Selling Digital Products 2025](https://www.etsy.com/market/best_selling_digital_products_2025)

## Licensing constraint for Canva-based products

Canva states that products for sale must be original designs and that Canva content cannot be resold on a standalone basis. It allows templates, print products, and e-books under its license conditions. Designs containing Canva Pro content may be sold as Canva template links, rather than as editable PDF or other exported template files. Digital clipart for use outside Canva cannot be made from Canva Free or Pro content; such clipart needs to be created from scratch.

Source: [Canva: Use Canva to design digital and physical products for sale](https://www.canva.com/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/)
